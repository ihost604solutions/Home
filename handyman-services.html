Repair · Assemble · Mount

Inside Your Home

TV Mounting & Wall Installation

TV brackets, shelves, mirrors, picture frames and curtain rods. We assess the wall material, mounting points and item weight to confirm suitable fixings and placement.

Furniture & IKEA Assembly

Assembly of beds, desks, wardrobes, dressers and other flat-pack furniture, with door and drawer adjustments and wall anchoring where required.

Doors, Cabinets & Hardware

Minor repairs and adjustments to interior doors, hinges, handles, locks, cabinet doors and drawer slides. Send photos and a short description of what is loose, sticking or damaged.

Drywall Patching & Paint Touch-Ups

Patching small holes and localised wall damage, sanding and paint touch-ups. Colour and sheen matching depend on the existing finish and available paint.

Caulking & Sealing

Removal and replacement of worn caulking around tubs, showers, sinks and countertops, after checking the surface condition. Underlying leaks or damaged surfaces require separate assessment.

Trim & Finishing Repairs

Localised baseboard and door-trim repairs or replacement, securing loose pieces and completing small finishing details.

Small Repairs & Maintenance Visits

Bring your home or rental-property repair list together: loose handles, sticking drawers, misaligned doors and items that need mounting. We confirm the agreed tasks before the visit.

Make more of your outdoor space

Gazebos, Sheds & Outdoor Storage

Gazebo Assembly

Assembly of prefabricated gazebo kits, including frame, roof components and anchoring appropriate to the kit and existing surface. Share the model, kit instructions and site photos so we can assess access, base suitability and installation requirements. Replacing an existing gazebo? Dismantling and removal of the old structure can be included in your quote.

Shed Assembly

Assembly of prefabricated garden and storage sheds, including panels, roof, doors and supplied hardware. The base must be suitable for the shed model; base preparation can be quoted separately. We can also quote dismantling and disposal of your old shed before assembling the replacement.

Shed Base Preparation

Assessment and preparation of a level support base for a prefabricated shed. Options such as a gravel or paver base or a timber platform depend on the site and manufacturer’s requirements. Excavation, drainage, concrete work and structural foundations are assessed separately.

Old Shed & Gazebo Dismantling, Removal & Disposal

Taking down an old shed or gazebo? We assess the structure, dismantle it in manageable sections, load and haul away the agreed materials, and clear the work area. Removal can be booked on its own or combined with replacement assembly.

Send photos, approximate dimensions, construction material and access details. Your quote will identify the dismantling work, hauling and disposal fees. Emptying stored contents, removing concrete bases or footings, and disconnecting utilities are assessed separately. Structures with suspected hazardous materials require specialist assessment before removal.

Garbage & Recycling Bin Enclosures

Assembly and installation of outdoor bin-storage boxes and enclosures to keep garbage and recycling containers organised. Send the product model or desired dimensions, bin sizes and a photo of the location; custom-built options are assessed individually.

Outdoor assembly depends on site conditions, the product and anchoring requirements. Any required property approvals or permits must be in place before work begins.

Refresh your workspace

Office Ceiling & Lighting Updates

Suspended Ceiling Tile Replacement — 2 × 2 & 2 × 4 ft

Replacement of damaged, stained or worn ceiling panels in existing suspended ceiling grids. We check panel dimensions, thickness, edge profile and grid condition to identify suitable replacement tiles, then remove the agreed panels and fit the new ones.

Share photos, ceiling height and an approximate panel count. Matching the existing finish depends on product availability. Any source of water staining should be resolved before new panels are fitted.

Office Light Fixture Replacement & LED Upgrades

Replacement of existing office light fixtures with compatible LED panels, troffers or other modern fixtures, including 2 × 2 and 2 × 4 ft formats. We review the existing fixture type, mounting, power supply, controls and desired light output and colour temperature before confirming the scope.

Quoted work can include removal of old fixtures, installation of compatible replacements and operation checks. Electrical work is assessed separately and must be carried out by appropriately licensed personnel, with permits where required. New circuits, rewiring and changes to emergency lighting are outside a standard fixture-replacement quote.

We can quote ceiling panels and lighting together for a coordinated office refresh. Let us know your preferred work hours, access arrangements and whether removal and disposal of old materials should be included.
