Replacement & Removal

Replacing an old appliance? Tell us whether you need it disconnected, removed or hauled away. We’ll include the agreed services in your quote.

What to Expect

We review the installation area, protect nearby surfaces, install and secure the appliance, check the connections and test its operation. Before leaving, we explain the basic operation and tidy the work area.

Before You Book

Send us:

  • Appliance brand and model number
  • Photos of the installation area and existing connections
  • Property location and preferred installation date
  • Whether an old appliance needs removal
  • Any stairs, tight access or stacking requirements

Important Installation Details

Our standard installations use suitable existing connections. Cabinet changes, new plumbing, new electrical connections and additional parts require a separate assessment. We do not install or connect gas appliances.
