A precise fit for your kitchen

Built-In & Integrated Appliance Installation

Built-in appliances need the right fit, clearances and connections. We review the model, cabinet opening, mounting requirements and manufacturer’s installation instructions to confirm the scope before your appointment.

Built-In & Integrated Refrigerators

Installation and replacement of built-in, integrated and panel-ready refrigerators and freezers. Scope may include positioning, levelling, securing, door-panel alignment and connection to an existing compatible water supply, depending on the model.

Built-In & Panel-Ready Dishwashers

Installation into existing cabinet openings, connection to suitable existing water, drain and power, levelling, securing and operation checks. Decorative door panels are fitted where compatible with the appliance and supplied hardware.

Built-In Coffee Machines

Installation of built-in coffee and espresso machines into suitable cabinet openings. Includes securing the appliance and checking access, ventilation and existing connections. Plumbed models require a compatible existing water supply.

Wall Ovens & Built-In Microwaves

Installation of single and double wall ovens, combination ovens, built-in microwaves and compatible trim kits. Cabinet dimensions, ventilation clearances and electrical requirements are reviewed before booking.

Electric & Induction Cooktops

Replacement installation into compatible existing countertop cutouts, with suitable existing electrical connections. Share the new model and cutout measurements so we can assess the fit.

Warming Drawers & Specialty Built-In Appliances

Installation assessment for warming drawers, built-in wine coolers, beverage centres and undercounter appliances. Send the model and cabinet measurements so we can confirm compatibility and scope.

Kitchen & bathroom upgrades

Kitchen & Bathroom Fixture Installation

Replacing a fixture or refreshing a bathroom? We install toilets, sinks, faucets, garburators and vanities using suitable existing connections, with the work and any additional parts agreed in advance.

Toilets

Removal and replacement of standard floor-mounted toilets, including checking the existing flange and supply connection, fitting the appropriate seal, securing the fixture and testing for leaks and flushing operation. Wall-hung models are assessed individually for compatibility with the existing carrier and tank.

Kitchen & Bathroom Sinks

Installation and replacement of kitchen sinks and bathroom basins, including compatible drain, trap and supply connections and sealing where required. Sink style, countertop opening and mounting support are reviewed before quoting.

Kitchen & Bathroom Faucets

Replacement of kitchen and bathroom faucets, including compatible supply-line connections, securing, sealing where required, and checks for leaks and water flow. Share the faucet model and mounting-hole layout.

Garburators / Garbage Disposals

Removal and replacement of under-sink food-waste disposers. Includes compatible mounting, drain reconnection, dishwasher drain connection where applicable, and operation and leak checks using suitable existing connections.

Bathroom Vanities

Installation and replacement of bathroom vanity units, with positioning, levelling and securing, plus compatible sink, faucet and drain connections as agreed. Wall-mounted units, countertop fit and any required backing or plumbing changes are assessed before booking.

Replacement & Removal

Replacing an old appliance or fixture? Tell us whether you need it disconnected, removed or hauled away. We’ll include the agreed services in your quote.

What to Expect

We review the installation area, protect nearby surfaces, install and secure the appliance or fixture, check the connections and test its operation. Before leaving, we explain the basic operation and tidy the work area.

Before You Book

Send us:

  • Appliance or fixture brand and model number
  • Photos of the installation area and existing connections
  • Property location and preferred installation date
  • Whether an old appliance or fixture needs removal
  • Cabinet or countertop opening dimensions for built-in appliances
  • Any stairs, tight access, stacking or wall-mounting requirements

Important Installation Details

Our standard installations use suitable existing connections. Cabinet changes, new plumbing, new electrical connections and additional parts require a separate assessment. We do not install or connect gas appliances.

Convenience for your garage

Garage Door Opener Installation

Installation and replacement of compatible residential garage door openers, including the motor unit, rail, mounting hardware, wall control and safety sensors. We review the door type, size, headroom, existing power supply and product requirements before quoting.

Setup & Operation Checks

Setup of travel limits and force settings according to the manufacturer’s instructions, remote programming and checks of the safety sensors and reversing function. Compatible smart features can be configured where supported by the model and your home network.

Before Installation

The garage door must be balanced and operate correctly by hand. Spring, cable, track and door repairs are outside the opener-installation scope and need to be resolved before fitting the opener. Send the opener model and photos of the door, ceiling and existing installation.
