Before You Hand Over the Keys

Move-Out Walkthrough

Before you return the keys, we walk through your home and identify visible issues that may need attention. Get a clear task list and a quote for small fixes to help reduce avoidable deposit deductions.

Minor Repairs & Paint Touch-Ups

Small wall-hole patching, minor surface repairs, paint touch-ups and adjustments to loose handles, hinges and cabinet doors. Targeted repairs quoted to suit the work needed.

Appliance Condition Checks

Basic operational checks of kitchen and laundry appliances, with a written summary of visible issues and observed performance. Useful when preparing a home for sale, rental turnover or handover.

Kitchen & Bathroom Fixture Checks

Checks of accessible faucets, sinks, toilets and visible plumbing connections for leaks, drainage issues and basic operation.

Photo Reports & Handover Preparation

A concise report with photos, findings, completed repairs and recommended next steps. Cleaning can be added to prepare the property for handover.

Condition checks document accessible areas and observed operation; they are not a certified home inspection or a guarantee of deposit return.
