01 · Commercial spaces

Office & Commercial Cleaning

Regular and deep cleaning for offices, retail stores and commercial spaces. Keep your workplace welcoming for staff, customers and visitors.

Book a one-time visit or arrange recurring cleaning tailored to your space. Share your opening hours and preferred cleaning times so we can confirm a suitable schedule.

Service scope

  • Floors and accessible surfaces
  • Washrooms and staff kitchens
  • Entrance areas and interior glass
  • Waste-bin emptying

02 · A more detailed clean

Deep Cleaning

For spaces that need more than routine maintenance. We give extra attention to buildup and overlooked areas, with the scope agreed upon before booking.

Tell us about the size and current condition of the space, priority areas and any surfaces that need special care.

Service scope

  • Corners and accessible baseboards
  • Detailed kitchen and washroom cleaning
  • Frequently touched surfaces
  • Accessible areas needing additional attention

03 · A fresh start

Move-In & Move-Out Cleaning

Thorough cleaning of vacant homes, offices and retail spaces before moving in or after moving out.

Share your handover date and whether the property will be empty. We confirm the cleaning checklist before the visit.

Service scope

  • Inside empty cupboards and drawers
  • Kitchens and bathrooms
  • Floors and accessible surfaces
  • Interior appliance cleaning when included in your quote

04 · Shared spaces

Building & Common Area Cleaning

Scheduled or one-time cleaning of shared spaces for property managers and strata buildings.

We plan the cleaning frequency, access arrangements and priorities around your building’s needs.

Service scope

  • Lobbies and entrance areas
  • Hallways and stairwells
  • Shared washrooms
  • Other agreed common areas

05 · Exterior care

Gutter Cleaning

Removal of leaves and debris from accessible gutters to help keep rainwater flowing.

Quotes depend on building height, access, gutter length and condition. Send exterior photos and details of any overflowing areas. Repairs and inaccessible or underground blockages are assessed separately.

Service scope

  • Removal of gutter leaves and debris
  • Clearing accessible downspout openings
  • Water-flow checks where practical
  • Bagging collected debris and tidying the work area

Regular or One-Time Cleaning

Yes—send your full task list so we can assess the work, time and materials together.
