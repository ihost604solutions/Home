Privacy & Quote Requests

Updated November 22, 2026.

What you share

Our embedded Tally form collects your name, telephone number, email, service address, job description and optional photos and preferred date. Tally processes and stores the submission for iHost and sends an email notification to ihost604@gmail.com. Photo access links may be included in that notification. Avoid including identity documents or unrelated private information in photos.

How we use enquiries

We use these details to assess your request, prepare a quote, contact you and coordinate agreed services. Submitting a request does not confirm a booking.

External services

Tally, Google Maps and Instagram have their own privacy practices. Review excerpts on our website are a saved selection, not a live review feed.

Tally privacy policy

Website measurement

Google Analytics is used to understand visits to our website. Google Tag Manager manages measurement tags and may record non-sensitive actions such as opening a quote form, clicking a phone or WhatsApp link, and successfully submitting our embedded Tally quotation form. These website events do not include the customer’s name, email address, telephone number, service address, message text or uploaded photos. A submitted quotation request is not a confirmed booking.

Your enquiry

For questions or to request deletion of information you provided, email ihost604@gmail.com.
