Privacy & Quote Requests

Updated November 14, 2026.

What you share

The quote form prepares an email containing the information you enter. It does not automatically send or store your request on this website. You review and send it using your email app. Attach photos only if they help explain the work; avoid personal documents and unrelated private details.

How we use enquiries

We use the contact information and job details you send to assess your request, reply and coordinate agreed services. Your email provider handles the email you choose to send.

External services

Links to Google Maps, Instagram and our published review feed open external services with their own privacy practices. Review excerpts on this website are a saved selection, not a live feed.

Website measurement

The website prepares anonymous interaction events in page memory, such as a call-button click. This version does not send those events to an analytics provider. Contact details and message contents are not included in those events.

Contact

For questions about your enquiry or to request removal of information you sent, email info@ihost604.ca or call 604-305-5050.
